Support — Whisper Speak

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Whisper Speak records, transcribes, labels speakers, summarizes, and translates — entirely on your iPhone. This page covers the questions that come up most, and is honest about where automatic features have limits.

Getting started

Tap the mic on the Home tab and start speaking; tap Done when you finish. To transcribe an existing audio file (m4a, mp3, wav…), use the Import tab. Everything is saved to History automatically, and nothing needs an account or an internet connection.

Speaker labels

How do speaker labels work?

Whisper Speak tells voices apart on-device and labels their turns "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", and so on. Tap any speaker's name in a transcript to rename them.

The app split one person into two speakers — or mixed two people up

Automatic speaker detection is good but not perfect; its most common miss is splitting one person into two labels across a long silence. The fix is built in: tap the speaker's name and choose Merge. If a merge turns out to be wrong, Restore Original Speakers (on the transcript screen) puts back what was originally detected. Nothing is ever lost to a wrong guess.

Can I turn speaker labels off?

Yes — Settings → Label Speakers. With it off, recordings transcribe faster as plain text. Existing transcripts keep their labels either way.

Translation

How does translation work?

Translation runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device translation. Picking a language marked with a download arrow fetches that language once; your transcript never leaves the device. The original transcript is always kept — switch between versions above the text.

The translation reads oddly in places

On-device translation handles clear sentences well but can be literal with idioms and casual speech ("I've gone off soups" may not survive the trip). Two tips: speech that transcribed cleanly translates better, and the original is always there to check against. For critical documents, treat the translation as a strong draft.

Transcription

The transcript came out in the wrong language

Set the language in Settings → Language, then use Transcribe Again from the transcript's ⋯ menu — it re-runs the full model over your saved audio.

My transcript is empty

If no speech was detected, the transcript stays empty rather than inventing words. If you expected words, check the language setting and try Transcribe Again. If a recording was interrupted (a call, a force-quit), it appears as a Recovered recording with its audio saved — Transcribe runs the full model over it.

Which transcription engines are there?

Apple Speech runs instantly with no download. Whisper covers more languages and needs a one-time download of about 630 MB. Switch in Settings → Engine. The optional on-device summarizer is a separate one-time download (about 880 MB); both can be removed again in Settings.

Your data

Deleting recordings

Deleting a recording removes the transcript and its audio from your iPhone permanently — there is no trash to recover from, which is why every delete asks first.

Exporting

Share any transcript as plain text, PDF, or Word — and the audio itself — from the share button on the transcript screen.

Requirements

Whisper Speak runs on iPhone 11 and newer with iOS 26.5 or later. iPhone only — no iPad or Mac version at this time.
