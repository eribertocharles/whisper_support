Support — Whisper Speak

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Whisper Speak records, transcribes, labels speakers, summarizes, and translates — entirely on your iPhone. This page answers the most common questions. It is also honest about the mistakes speaker labels and translations can make — and how to fix them.

Getting started

Tap the mic on the Home tab and start speaking; tap Done when you finish. To transcribe a recording you already have — a voice memo, or an audio file someone sent you — use the Import tab. All the common audio types (m4a, mp3, wav) work. Everything is saved to History automatically. There is no account to create, and no internet connection needed.

Speaker labels

How do speaker labels work?

Whisper Speak tells voices apart and labels their turns "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", and so on — all on your iPhone. Tap any speaker's name in a transcript to rename them.

The app split one person into two speakers — or mixed two people up

Automatic speaker detection is good but not perfect. Its most common mistake is giving one person two labels when there is a long pause between their sentences. The fix is built in: tap the speaker's name and choose Merge. If a merge turns out to be wrong, Restore Original Speakers (on the transcript screen) puts back what was first detected. Nothing is ever lost to a wrong guess.

Can I turn speaker labels off?

Yes — Settings → Label Speakers. With it off, recordings transcribe faster as plain text. Existing transcripts keep their labels either way.

Translation

How does translation work?

Translation happens on your iPhone, using Apple's translation. If a language shows a download arrow, choosing it downloads that language to your phone one time — after that it works without internet. Your transcript never leaves the device, and the original is always kept — switch between versions above the text.

The translation reads oddly in places

Translation on your phone handles clear sentences well. But it can translate idioms and casual speech word for word — a phrase like "I've gone off soups" may come out with the wrong meaning. Two tips: speech that transcribed cleanly translates better, and the original is always there to check against. For important documents, treat the translation as a strong first draft.

Transcription

The transcript came out in the wrong language

Set the language in Settings → Language, then use Transcribe Again from the transcript's ⋯ menu. It transcribes your saved audio again from the beginning, in the language you chose.

My transcript is empty

If no speech was detected, the transcript stays empty rather than inventing words. If you expected words, check the language setting and try Transcribe Again. If a recording was cut short — by a phone call, or because the app was closed mid-recording — it appears as a Recovered recording with its audio saved. Transcribe turns that saved audio into a full transcript.

Which transcription engines are there?

Apple Speech runs instantly with no download. Whisper covers more languages and needs a one-time download of about 630 MB. Switch in Settings → Engine. The optional summarizer is a separate one-time download (about 880 MB). You can remove Whisper or the summarizer at any time in Settings.

Your data

Deleting recordings

Deleting a recording removes the transcript and its audio from your iPhone permanently. There is no trash folder, so a deleted recording cannot be brought back. That is why the app always asks you to confirm first.

Exporting

Share any transcript as plain text, PDF, or Word — and the audio itself — from the share button on the transcript screen.

Why does everything stay on your iPhone?

Most transcription services upload your audio to their servers. Whisper Speak keeps everything on your iPhone, and that is a design decision, not a limitation. Because your audio never leaves your phone, no one can steal it from a server, demand it in court, or use it to train AI. Transcription works anywhere — on a plane, in places with no signal, in secure buildings. And there is no account, because there is nothing to sign into.

How does accuracy compare to cloud AI?

Honestly: internet-based services can use far bigger computers than any phone, and some identify speakers by reading your calendar and meeting apps, not just the audio. For clear recordings, the transcription Whisper Speak does on your phone is about as accurate as the big cloud services. Speaker labels are where you may sometimes notice a difference — telling similar voices apart on a far-away microphone is the hardest problem in this field. That is why every label can be fixed with a tap: merge, rename, restore. This is a choice we made on purpose. Cloud services get their accuracy by using your audio. With Whisper Speak, your audio stays with you.

Requirements

Whisper Speak runs on iPhone 11 and newer with iOS 26.5 or later. iPhone only — there is no iPad or Mac version at this time.
